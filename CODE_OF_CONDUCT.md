# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming and harassment-free community for everyone.
Participation is expected to be respectful, constructive, and professional.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful in language and feedback.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions.
- Focus on ideas and technical outcomes, not individuals.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Help make collaboration accessible to all contributors.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination based on personal identity or background.
- Deliberate intimidation or hostile behavior.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Repeated disruption of discussions or review processes.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and review comments.
It also applies to public representation of the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer at shane.wilkins@gmail.com.
Please include relevant context and links when possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments and contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.
